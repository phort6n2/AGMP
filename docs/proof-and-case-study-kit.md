## 4) Proof-Collection Checklist (by impact)
1. **Written consent** to name the shop + use a quote (nothing publishes without it).
2. **Booked-jobs count, before & after** (the number that closes deals).
3. **Owner pull-quote** verbatim ("What changed, and what would you tell another owner?").
4. **Map Pack before/after screenshots** (dated, same keyword/city).
5. **Call-volume screenshot** (GBP Insights 6-month graph or call tracking).
6. **Cash-vs-insurance mix shift** (margin story).
7. **ADAS calibration jobs captured** (high-ticket — its own callout).
8. **A photo of the owner / shop / a truck** (real faces beat logos).
9. **Google review count/rating lift.**
10. **Timeframe on everything** ("in 90 days" makes numbers believable).

**Consent snippet:** "Hey {{name}} — we'd love to feature {{Shop}}'s results on our site. OK to use your shop name, a short quote, and your before/after numbers? You can review before it goes live and pull it any time. Reply YES."
